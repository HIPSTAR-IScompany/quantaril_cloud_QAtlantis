## Forks and proprietary products

Apache-2.0 code may be forked and used in closed or proprietary products under its terms. CC BY mythology and worldbuilding material may be reused under its terms. Independent SDKs, platforms, models, and proprietary games are allowed.

A closed product may truthfully state that it uses or derives from Q Atlantis. It does not automatically receive official lineage, orthodoxy, canonical compatibility, or lineage-compatible MAGI status.

A bundled system claiming that form of authority must open the complete reproducible basis of the claim under the ShareAlike lineage policy, including its actual model, prompts, audit and quarantine logic, internal implementation, tools, data, tests, provenance, and build conditions.

## Sacred NSFW is not a disposable tier

Q Atlantis does not treat lawful NSFW, erotic, bodily, fertility-related, comic, festive, or ritual expression as inferior to SFW material.

Age gates, consent controls, jurisdictional separation, labels, access boundaries, payment routes, and safety controls may be implemented. Delivery constraints do not authorize sanitizing or erasing the source mythology.
