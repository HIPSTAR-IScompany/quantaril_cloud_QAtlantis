## Q Atlantis integration boundary

Q Atlantis applies those policies as follows:

1. Code and SDK components retain their file-level licenses, normally Apache-2.0 where marked.
2. Mythology, world flavor, prose, and diagrams retain their file-level licenses, normally CC BY 4.0 where marked.
3. Black-box models, platforms, SDKs, clouds, and proprietary games may use or derive from the public materials under the applicable licenses.
4. Descriptive attribution such as `built with`, `uses`, `derived from`, or `inspired by` does not require lineage certification.
5. Claims of official lineage, orthodoxy, canonical compatibility, or lineage-compatible MAGI status require a fully reproducible open bundle under the ShareAlike lineage policy.
6. The reproducible bundle includes the actual model and model version, prompts, audit and quarantine rules, internal implementation, tools, retrieval sources, schemas, tests, provenance, build conditions, and human override paths.
7. The upstream Q Atlantis project remains model-independent and does not bundle one mandatory sacred model.
8. A closed implementation without a lineage claim is an independent product or world, not an invalid product.
9. Support and stewardship do not purchase exclusivity, official authority, mythology ownership, or control of the upstream roadmap.
10. Lawful NSFW, sacred eroticism, bodily ritual, fertility, comedy, and festival material remain equal cultural branches. Access and jurisdiction controls may separate delivery without downgrading the source tradition.

## Honest status

These policies define governance and participation boundaries. They do not imply that Q Atlantis is a completed operating system, that a financial instrument is currently open, or that any contribution will receive media exposure or a mythological role.

Any actual donation, grant, investment, equity, debt, revenue share, tax treatment, or regulated offering requires a separate written instrument and the appropriate legal and accounting entity.
