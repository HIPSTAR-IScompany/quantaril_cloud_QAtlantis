# OpenFAPTA overview

This en-US text is a reference manuscript of the current overview in the [canonical Japanese page](intro.md). Consult Japanese when scope or interpretation differs. The historical posts on that page are preserved and are not translated or rewritten here.

OpenFAPTA is an open, general-purpose merchant-engine design for credit creation through recovery potential and the exploration frontier of unknown commons. Its users register candidates, try and retry under explicit conditions, discover new unknowns, and update remaining recovery and exploration opportunities.

The proposed M-dimensional portfolio retains frontier extent, volatility, recovery conditions, and cross-Fold resource effects. Users select Fold topology, area measures, evaluation units, and allocation policies according to their goals. The configuration principle can be applied recursively across communities and currency issuers.

Currency, exchange units, balances, settlement, and distributed ledgers are external responsibilities. Cryptocurrency, stablecoin, voucher, or points implementations can be selected by an integrator; no such adapter is currently shipped in the Python toys.

The code currently covers resource activation, accounting visibility, unknown benefits, and exploration states. General charts, persistent candidate/retry management, and cross-Fold positive-sum detection remain design and implementation work.
